Privacy Policy

Money Coach · Last updated 2026-09-08

TL;DR — Your financial ledger stays on your phone. No ads and no product-analytics SDK. Production builds send crash reports and performance traces to Sentry so we can fix stability issues; sensitive financial content is redacted and not uploaded. Optional Premium uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat only to verify purchases — not to upload your money history.

1. Who runs Money Coach

Money Coach is a personal-finance education app made by an independent developer. Contact us via the email on the Google Play Store listing.

2. The short version

What you might worry aboutWhat actually happens
Does the app collect my financial ledger?No Accounts, transactions, goals, and balances stay on your phone.
Does it send my money data to a Money Coach server?No There is no ledger backend.
Do I need to create an account?No No sign-up for the core app.
Are there ads or product-analytics trackers?No No advertising SDK and no product-analytics SDK. Crash and performance diagnostics go to Sentry.
Do you send crash reports?Yes Production builds send crash reports and performance traces to Sentry to fix stability issues. Ledger contents are scrubbed and not uploaded.
What about Premium?Purchases go through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat verifies entitlements using billing identifiers — not your transaction history.

3. What stays on your device

  • Accounts, transactions, goals, budget rules, gamification, preferences
  • App PIN — stored as a salted hash verifier in secure device storage (not plaintext)
  • Optional Premium cache flag synced with Play / RevenueCat when online

Android Auto Backup is disabled for app data so your finance ledger is not included in automatic Google device backups. You can still export a backup from Settings.

4. Permissions

Notifications — local reminders only (no push server).

Camera — optional receipt photos; stay on-device.

Photos — optional goal covers / receipts. On Android the app uses the system Photo Picker and does not request broad READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.

5. Third parties

No advertising SDKs, product-analytics SDKs, or attribution SDKs. Production builds use Sentry for crash and performance diagnostics. Optional Premium also uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.

Crash / stability monitoring (Sentry): Production Android and iOS builds send crash reports and performance traces to Sentry (@sentry/react-native) so we can diagnose crashes and fix stability issues. Sensitive financial content is redacted on the device before a report is sent. This does not upload your accounts, transactions, goals, balances, notes, PIN codes, or backup file contents. Sentry may receive crash logs, diagnostic information (error type, file names, line numbers), performance traces, and app or device identifiers used to group crashes. Traffic uses HTTPS.

If you buy or restore Premium:

  • Google Play Billing processes payment
  • RevenueCat verifies entitlements (purchase tokens, subscription status, device/app identifiers needed for billing verification)
  • Your ledger is not uploaded

6. Educational disclaimer

Money Coach is an educational and personal-tracking tool — not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

7. Your rights

Export or reset data from Settings, or uninstall. Manage Premium subscriptions in Google Play.

8. Contact

Use the developer email on the Money Coach Play Store listing.
