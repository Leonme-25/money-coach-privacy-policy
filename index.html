Privacy Policy

Money Coach · Last updated 2026-05-21

TL;DR — Money Coach is an offline-first finance app. Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, goals, balances, PIN — stays on your device. We don't have servers, we don't have your data, and we never will.

1. Who runs Money Coach

Money Coach is a personal-finance education app made by an independent developer ("we", "us", "our"). The app is provided as-is for educational and personal-tracking purposes.

If you need to reach us, please contact us via the email address listed on the Google Play Store listing.

2. The short version

What you might worry aboutWhat actually happens
Does the app collect my financial data?No Nothing leaves your phone.
Does it send my data to a server?No There is no backend. The app makes zero network calls for your personal data.
Do I need to create an account?No No sign-up, no email, no phone number.
Are there ads or trackers?No No analytics SDK, no ad network, no third-party tracking.
Do you sell my data?No We have no data to sell.
Can the developer see my balances?No Your accounts and transactions are stored on your device only.

3. What data Money Coach stores ON YOUR DEVICE

You enter this data yourself. It is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. We do not have access to it.

  • Accounts — names, types (cash/bank/wallet), currency, balances, notes
  • Transactions — amounts, categories, dates, optional notes
  • Goals — names, target amounts, monthly contribution, optional photo
  • Budget rule selection (e.g. 50/30/20)
  • Gamification state — XP, streak count, unlocked badges
  • Preferences — your chosen name, avatar emoji, theme, haptics, notification settings
  • App PIN (if you enable it) — stored using your device's secure enclave (expo-secure-store, Keychain on iOS / Keystore on Android)

You can wipe ALL of this in one tap via Settings → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

4. What data Money Coach does NOT store

  • Your real name, email, phone number, address, government ID, or any identifier
  • Your bank login, card numbers, or any credentials
  • Your location
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your microphone or camera feed (no recording)
  • Any data on a server — there is no server

5. Permissions Money Coach requests

The app requests only the minimum needed to function. You can deny any of these and the app will keep working (with reduced features).

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Why: To send you local reminders that you schedule — daily money check-ins, streak nudges, and goal-progress prompts.
What happens: All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Money Coach has no push-notification server.
Opt-out: Settings → Notifications master toggle, or via your phone's system settings.

Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES)

Why: So you can attach a personal photo to a savings goal (e.g. a phone you want to buy, a place you want to visit). It's purely visual motivation.
What happens: The photo is read from your library and stored in the app's local storage as a small base64 thumbnail. The photo never leaves your device.
Opt-out: Deny the prompt; goals will use an emoji instead.

6. Third parties

Money Coach contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, telemetry, or attribution SDKs.

We use a handful of standard open-source Expo modules for the app's UI and functionality (e.g. expo-router, expo-notifications, expo-secure-store, expo-image-picker). These modules execute entirely on your device and do not transmit your data anywhere.

7. Children's privacy

Money Coach is designed for general audiences (recommended 13+). We do not knowingly collect any data — from anyone — so we have no children's data to mishandle.

8. Educational disclaimer

Money Coach is an educational and personal-tracking tool. It is not financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, or legal advice. The financial simulators use standard textbook formulas and are clearly labelled "Educational only" in-app. Always consult a licensed professional before making real financial decisions.

9. Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete or transfer. You own your data, on your phone. You can:

  • Export a PDF report from Settings → Export
  • Reset all data in one tap from Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and announce the change in-app and in the Play Store release notes. The current architecture is intentionally offline-first.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, please reach us via the developer email shown on the Money Coach Play Store listing.
